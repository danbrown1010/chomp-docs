Trip Planning

Pre-departure intelligence: routes, conditions, weather, fuel, and home automation.

Offline Map Tiles + GPX Routes
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Download Caltopo / OsmAnd tiles and custom GPX layers before departure. Caches the active trip area at multiple zoom levels for full off-grid navigation.

  • Recommended zoom levels 10–16 for PNW forest roads
  • Import GPX from Caltopo, Gaia GPS, or custom tracks
  • Cache budget: 500MB–2GB for typical PNW trip area
Campsite & Waypoint Planner
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Pin campsites, water sources, bailout points, and POIs with notes and elevation. Sync from iOverlander and FreeCampsites pre-trip; view fully offline.

Trip Journal with GPS Track Logging
P1 — Core🔵 Off-grid

Background GPS breadcrumb recording. Auto-generates daily track logs. Export as GPX or KML post-trip for Lightroom, Google Earth, or sharing.

Burn Ban Status Card
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Pulls county-level burn restrictions from AirNow, InciWeb, and WA DNR before departure. Caches last-known status with a prominent "last updated" timestamp.

Weather Window Planner
P2 — High Value⚪ On-grid

NOAA / OpenMeteo forecasts for trip dates. Flags high-wind or precipitation windows relevant to rooftop camping with the Ursa Minor. Wind >35mph sustained: rooftop camping not recommended.

Road Condition & Permit Tracker
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Store road status notes per route with reminder dates for permits. Common PNW permits: Northwest Forest Pass, Discover Pass, Okanogan-Wenatchee NF campfire permits.

Fuel & Range Calculator
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Pulls highway and offroad MPG from your vehicle profile. Enter start fuel level and route distance — estimates fuel needed with configurable reserve margin. Flags nearest gas station pre-departure. All values editable per trip.

Home Assistant Departure Automation
P3 — Nice to Have⚪ On-grid

Trip start triggers HA webhook: arm security, set thermostat to away, turn off lights. See Architecture → Home Assistant Integration for setup.

Safety & Conditions

Live situational awareness before and during the trip. Fire perimeter GeoJSON (~5–15MB for western US) is cached at trip-download time.

Active Fire Perimeter Map
P1 — Core🟢 Both

NIFC / InciWeb GeoJSON overlay on the offline base map. Pre-cached at trip download. Shows active fire boundaries relative to GPS position and planned route. Source: NIFC ArcGIS, updated daily on-grid.

Evacuation Zone Status
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Displays current evacuation zone level for your GPS position. Cached pre-trip; refreshes on reconnect.

LevelColorMeaning
Advisorycircle YellowBe aware, be ready
Warningcircle OrangeBe set to go immediately
Ordercircle RedLeave now
Burn Ban Status by County
P1 — Core🟢 Both

County-level burn restriction status from WA DNR and AirNow. Cached with timestamp. Also surfaced in Trip Planning pre-departure card.

AQI & Smoke Forecast Layer
P2 — High Value⚪ On-grid

Air quality index and smoke plume overlay. AQI <100: clear shooting conditions for all rigs. AQI 100–150: haze visible in footage — affects D850, D70s, and aerial. AQI >150: fly Air 2s with caution, Wolfbox dash cam footage quality degraded.

Escape Route Planner
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Pre-download alternate exit GPX routes from each campsite. Surfaces fastest paved exit in one tap. Best practice: download two exit routes per camp in different directions — PNW single-canyon access roads can be cut off quickly.

Push Alerts for New Fires
P2 — High Value⚪ On-grid

Notify when a new fire ignition or perimeter expansion is detected within a configurable radius of the trip area (default: 25 miles).

NOAA Red Flag Warning Integration
P3 — Nice to Have⚪ On-grid

Surface active Red Flag Warnings (high wind + low humidity) for the trip area. Informs campfire decisions even where no formal ban is in effect. Source: NOAA NWS API.

Vehicle Maintenance

Configurable vehicle profile with internet-sourced maintenance schedules, mod registry, and pre-trip inspection.

Vehicle-agnostic by design: Maintenance schedules are fetched from manufacturer data and overlanding community sources based on your year, make, and model. No hardcoded intervals — every schedule is tailored to your actual rig.
Vehicle Profile
P1 — Core⚪ On-grid

Configure your rig with year, make, model, trim, engine, and drivetrain. Supports multiple vehicles per household. Each vehicle gets its own maintenance log, inspection checklist, and mod registry. Vehicle data drives the fuel calculator, load ledger, and maintenance schedule.

Recommended Maintenance Schedule
P1 — Core⚪ On-grid

On vehicle profile creation, Chomp fetches the recommended maintenance schedule from manufacturer service documentation, NHTSA data, and trusted overlanding community sources (iOverlander, Expedition Portal, Rock Auto). Schedule is cached locally for offline reference and reviewed by the user before activation.

  • Sources: OEM owner manual data, NHTSA service bulletins, community-validated offroad intervals
  • Offroad modifier: Shorten diff fluid intervals by a configurable % for heavy wheeling use
  • User review: All fetched intervals shown for confirmation before saving — you own your schedule
  • Manual override: Any interval can be adjusted and flagged as custom
  • Refresh: Re-fetch on demand when new TSBs or community data is available

Service Interval Reminders
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Mileage- and time-based alerts derived from your vehicle schedule. Push notification when approaching threshold (default: 500mi / 30 days before due). Upcoming reminders surface automatically in the pre-trip checklist.

Pre-Trip Vehicle Inspection Checklist
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Timestamped pass/fail walkaround per trip. Core items are universal; vehicle-specific items are added from your vehicle profile and mod registry. Generates a PDF record for insurance.

  • Tire PSI — all positions including spare (target PSI from vehicle profile)
  • Engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, washer fluid
  • All exterior lights functional
  • Recovery gear secured and accessible
  • Camper top / roof rack load secure
  • Any overdue service intervals flagged inline
Mod & Build Registry
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Catalog every modification with install date, parts, vendor, torque specs, and photos. Mods can add vehicle-specific items to the pre-trip checklist automatically (e.g. adding a locker adds a locker function check).

Tire Tracking & Rotation Log
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Track individual tires per position with tread depth, age, brand, and rotation history. Rotation pattern configurable: 4-tire or 5-tire with full-size spare.

Trail Damage & Incident Log
P3 — Nice to Have🔵 Off-grid

Log rock strikes, water crossings, recovery events, and tire events with GPS pin and photo. Useful for warranty claims and post-trip inspection focus areas.

Media Logging

Log a shot in 3 taps: select rig, tag subject, auto-save with GPS + timestamp. No photo upload — pure metadata. Matches to Lightroom catalog by timestamp post-trip.

  • Rigs: D850 / D70s / Air 2s / Insta360 / Wolfbox / iPhone 17 Pro
  • Subjects: landscape, wildlife, camp, vehicle, people
  • Optional: focal length, lighting conditions, notes

Per-flight records for post-production context and recreational best-practice logging.

  • Launch GPS, duration, subject, battery used

Pin locations with golden hour timing, best shooting direction, obstacles, and access notes. Resurfaces automatically on return trips to the same area.

Post-Trip Media Sync Queue

Home Assistant Integration

Keep scope narrow for v1: departure and return-home webhooks only. Two automations in HA, configured once, covers 90% of the value.

Avoid building a full HA dashboard inside Chomp — that's a deep rabbit hole with diminishing returns for an overlanding tool.

Meal Planning

Off-grid meal recommendations, shopping lists, and nutrition planning for multi-day overlanding trips.

Off-grid first: All meal plans are designed around no-refrigeration or minimal-refrigeration cooking. Recommendations account for your cooking setup, trip duration, number of people, and dietary preferences.
Trip Meal Planner
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Generate a complete meal plan for a trip based on duration, headcount, cooking setup, and dietary preferences. Meals are assigned to days and categorized by prep complexity. Fully viewable offline after generation.

InputExample
Trip duration5 days
People2 adults
Cooking setup2-burner propane, cast iron, no refrigeration
Dietary preferencesNo restrictions / vegetarian / gluten-free
Resupply stopsDay 3 — town with grocery store
Pets1 dog (auto-added from pet profile)
AI Meal Recommendations
P1 — Core⚪ On-grid

Claude-powered meal suggestions tailored to overlanding constraints: minimal water use, no refrigeration required, high calorie density for active days, simple one-pot prep. Suggestions account for elevation (affects boiling time), season, and available cooking gear.

  • Breakfast ideas: Oatmeal with dried fruit, breakfast burritos, granola + powdered milk
  • Lunch ideas: Tortillas + nut butter, hard cheese + crackers, summer sausage, no-cook options
  • Dinner ideas: One-pot pasta, freeze-dried backpacking meals, cast iron chili, foil packet meals
  • Snacks: Trail mix, jerky, energy bars, dried mango — calorie-dense, no prep
  • Recommendations cached after generation — available fully offline
Shopping List Generator
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Generates a consolidated, quantity-calculated shopping list from the meal plan. Items grouped by store section (produce, dry goods, meats, camping supplies). Accounts for resupply stops — splits list into pre-trip and mid-trip purchases. Shareable with your co-pilot before departure.

Meal Templates & Favourites
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Save meals you love as favourites. Build reusable meal plan templates per trip type (2-day summer, 5-day winter, photography mission). Favourites sync across household users so both of you can contribute to the meal rotation.

Cooking Setup Profile
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Configure your camp kitchen once — stove type, fuel (propane / butane / wood / alcohol), cookware (cast iron, titanium, nonstick), cooler type (none / ice / 12V fridge). Meal recommendations are filtered to what you can actually cook. Fuel consumption estimates added to the gear pack list.

  • Propane canister consumption estimated per meal plan
  • Cast iron flagged for weight in load ledger
  • 12V fridge detected from vehicle electrical mods if present
Calorie & Nutrition Tracker
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Estimate daily calorie targets based on trip activity level (light / moderate / heavy hiking and recovery work). Shows per-person daily totals for the meal plan. Useful for multi-day trips where energy management matters — especially at elevation in the PNW.

Community Recipe Library
P3 — Nice to Have⚪ On-grid

Community-contributed camp recipes with ratings, photos, and difficulty ratings. Filterable by cooking method, dietary restriction, and trip length. Submit your own recipes and build a reputation in the Chomp community.

Users & Identity

Authentication, profiles, and multi-tenant architecture. Designed for two users today, built to scale to a community tomorrow.

Current scope: Two users (Dan + wife) signing in with Gmail. Architecture designed for multi-tenant from day one so community rollout requires no re-engineering.
Google OAuth Sign-In
P1 — Core⚪ On-grid

Sign in with Google (Gmail identity). No passwords to manage. OAuth 2.0 via Firebase Auth or Supabase Auth — both support Google provider out of the box. Session persists offline after initial auth.

  • Initial sign-in requires network connection
  • Session token cached locally — stays signed in off-grid
  • Token refresh happens automatically on reconnect
  • Sign-out clears local session and cached data
User Profiles
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Each user has a profile with display name, avatar (from Google), preferred units, and their own camera kit configuration. Settings and checklists are per-user by default but can be shared within a household.

Household / Group
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Users belong to a household group. Shared data (trip plans, vehicle maintenance log, gear lists) is scoped to the household. Personal data (shot logs, camera kit) is scoped to the individual. The household is the base multi-tenant unit.

  • Current household: Dan + wife (2 users, 1 household)
  • Household owner can invite members via email
  • Shared data syncs across all household members
  • Role: Owner / Member (owner manages invites and billing)
Multi-Tenant Architecture
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

All data is scoped to a tenant ID from day one. A tenant is a household in the personal version, or an overlanding group / club in the community version. This means zero re-architecture is needed to roll Chomp out to the broader community — just enable tenant creation.

Offline Identity
P2 — High Value🔵 Off-grid

Once authenticated, the app works fully offline. User identity is cached locally. All writes are queued and synced on reconnect. If two users edit the same record offline, last-write-wins with a conflict log for review on reconnect.

Community Profiles
P3 — Nice to Have⚪ On-grid

Optional public profile for community members: display name, rig description, home region, trail reports contributed. Opt-in only — private by default. Enables community trail report attribution and reputation.

Pet Care

Optional module for overlanders travelling with pets. Activate per-user in profile settings.

Optional module: Hidden by default. Enable under Profile → My Pets. Adds a Pets tab to the main nav and surfaces pet-relevant reminders in trip planning.
Pet Profiles
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Register each pet with name, species, breed, age, weight, and photo. Supports multiple pets per household. Profile feeds into packing suggestions, vet record storage, and trip planning reminders.

  • Name, species, breed, age, weight
  • Microchip number (useful if lost in remote areas)
  • Emergency vet contact
  • Special needs / medications
Pet Pack List
P1 — Core🟢 Both

Dedicated checklist for pet gear. Merges into the main gear packing workflow. Templates per pet type (dog, cat). Fully offline.

  • Food and water (calculated by days × daily amount)
  • Collapsible bowls, leash, harness, ID tags
  • Bedding and crate (if applicable)
  • Medications and first aid (tick remover, wound care)
  • Waste bags, towels, paw protection
  • Vaccination records (digital copy)
Food & Water Calculator
P1 — Core🔵 Off-grid

Enter trip duration and number of pets. Calculates total food and water needed with a configurable safety buffer. Integrates with the main Water & Food Supply tracker so you're planning for the whole crew in one place.

Vet & Health Records
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Store vaccination records, medication schedules, and vet contact details per pet. Surfaces medication reminders during trip. Digital copy of rabies certificate useful at some campgrounds and border crossings.

Pet-Safe Trail Flags
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Flag waypoints and campsites as pet-friendly or hazardous. Hazard types: wildlife activity (bears, cougars, porcupines), toxic plants, extreme heat exposure, trail difficulty. Community-contributed, synced pre-trip.

Heat & Safety Alerts
P2 — High Value🟢 Both

Alerts when ambient temperature exceeds safe thresholds for pets left in the vehicle (even briefly). Uses weather forecast data pre-trip and device sensor data on-trip. Configurable per pet species and size.

Nearest Emergency Vet
P3 — Nice to Have⚪ On-grid

One-tap lookup of nearest emergency veterinary clinic from current GPS position. Pre-cache the closest options for the trip area before departure. Includes after-hours emergency lines.
